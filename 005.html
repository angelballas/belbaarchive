MOTHLIGHT I

2026
MOTHLIGHT I is the first of two instrumental albums selected from a larger collection of compositions. The division into two volumes emerged through repeated listening, with eleven pieces forming each sequence.

The first volume consists of short, sample-based compositions organised around slow hip-hop rhythms. Recurring drum patterns, overlapping melodic phrases, altered vocal fragments, and recorded noise form a dense sound in which individual elements are not always clearly distinguishable.

Produced in Athens, Greece.
